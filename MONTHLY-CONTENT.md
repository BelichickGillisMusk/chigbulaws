# Chigbu Law — monthly content (only playbook that matters)

## Once a month
1. Publish **one** blog post (third person, firm news — not Clifford’s “I/we are humbled” voice).
2. Share the post URL on **Facebook** (chigbulaw page).
3. Share the same post on **Google Business Profile**.

## Once a year
1. When a new award arrives: update homepage badge if needed + one recognition blog + FB + GBP.
2. He is humble — default to plain third-person facts, not a personal thank-you letter.

## Invoice gate
- Site loads; practice pages work; Contact + About open (no redirect loop).
- Current month’s post is live and shared.
- Annual award post is live when there is a new award (2026 post is the yearly recognition post).
