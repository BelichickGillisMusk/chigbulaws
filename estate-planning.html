What to bring to your consultation

For a will or trust

  • Any current will, trust, or power of attorney (copies are fine)
  • A simple list of what you own: home, vehicles, bank and retirement accounts, life insurance. Do not write out full account numbers.
  • Names of the people you would like to receive property, and to serve as executor, trustee, or guardian

If a loved one has passed

  • A copy of the death certificate
  • The will or trust, if there is one. Bring the original will in person. Never mail it, and do not write on it.
  • A list of property and debts you know about, such as a recent property tax bill or account statements

Common questions

Do I need a will or a living trust?

Many families benefit from both. A living trust lets property held in the trust pass to your loved ones without going through probate court. A will names a guardian for minor children and covers anything left outside the trust. Which one fits depends on what you own and who you want to protect.

What happens if someone dies without a will in California?

California law decides who inherits, usually starting with a spouse or registered domestic partner and children, then other relatives. A court appoints someone to manage the estate, and probate is often required.

What is probate and how long does it take?

Probate is the court process for paying a person's final debts and transferring their property. In Sacramento County it often takes about 9 to 18 months, and longer if there is a disagreement among family members.

Is probate always required?

No. Property in a living trust, property held in joint tenancy, and accounts with a named beneficiary usually pass without probate. Smaller estates, generally under $208,850 for deaths on or after April 1, 2025, may qualify for a simpler process without a full probate case.

How often should I update my will or trust?

Review it after a marriage, divorce, birth, adoption, or death in the family, after buying or selling a home, or after moving to California from another state. Otherwise, a review every few years is a good habit.
